Questions

How is this different from a normal AI chat?

A chatbot answers. A Bot does the work: it opens your apps in a real browser, clicks through them, fills things in, saves files and reports back. It also keeps a memory, runs on a schedule, and works while you're doing something else.

Is it actually free?

Yes. Tandem is MIT-licensed and has no paid tier. The AI comes from your own Claude Code plan, so every Bot reply counts toward that plan's usage limits like any other Claude Code session.

Do Bots share one computer?

Yes. All your Bots share one workspace folder and one browser profile on your Mac, so a sign-in done once works for all of them and they can hand files to each other. Isolation is per Mac account, not per Bot. Use a separate data folder for each client if you need them kept apart.

What happens when a site needs a password or 2FA?

Saved logins are filled in from your Mac Keychain, and the Bot never sees them. For passkeys, codes and CAPTCHAs, the Bot hands you the screen. You do that one step and press I'm done.

Can a Bot send an email or spend money without asking?

Not by default. Outgoing messages show up as drafts with a Send button, and anything that sends, buys, deletes or posts stops for your approval. You can loosen or tighten that with your own rules.

Does it work when my laptop is closed?

Bots run on your Mac, so they work while it's awake. Tandem starts at login and keeps going in the background with the window closed. For 24/7 routines, run it on a Mac mini that stays on.

Can I use it from my phone?

Yes. Start it with tandem install --lan, open the link from Settings → Devices on your phone, and add it to your home screen. It works like an app on the same Wi-Fi or over Tailscale.
