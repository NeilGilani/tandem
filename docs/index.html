Download for Mac

Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later. The app isn't notarized yet: the first time, right-click Tandem and choose Open.

1Install Claude Code and sign in once, if you haven't already.
2Open the app, or run the command on the right.
3Create your first Bot and tell it its job.
$ curl -fsSL …/install.sh | bash

The command needs Node 22.13+. Both need Claude Code. For a Linux laptop per Bot, also run brew install colima docker; otherwise Bots use Google Chrome on your Mac. Remove it anytime with tandem uninstall.

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Open an issue.

What is Tandem?

Tandem gives you AI Bots that work like coworkers. Each one has a job, a memory and a computer to work on. They sign in to your apps, do the work, and check with you before anything goes out.

Is it really free?

Yes. Tandem is MIT-licensed with no paid tier. The AI comes from your own Claude Code plan, so Bot replies count toward that plan's usage limits like any other Claude Code session.

Does a Bot do anything without asking me?

Only routine things, and only with a boss's OK. Bots with a boss send everyday email (supplier quotes, follow-ups, scheduling) once their boss Bot approves it. Anything where the impression matters, like investors, first contact or a commitment, comes to you with the reason why. Spending, signing and deleting your files always wait for you. You can tighten or loosen this with your own rules.

What is a squad, and does it cost more?

A squad is one job split across several helper Bots that work at the same time, each on its own computer. Your Bot decides when a job is big enough, or you can just ask for one. Each helper uses your Claude plan while it works, so a five-part squad uses it about five times as fast, for a fraction of the time.

Do Bots share a computer?

No. Every Bot gets its own Linux laptop with a desktop, Chromium, LibreOffice, Python and its own files. All of them run together in one small VM on your Mac (seven fit in 5 GB of memory), and a shared folder lets them hand work to each other. The laptops need Colima and Docker (brew install colima docker); without them, each Bot gets its own browser on your Mac instead.

What about passwords and 2FA?

Saved logins live in your Mac's Keychain and are filled in for the Bot, which never sees them. For passkeys, codes and CAPTCHAs, the Bot hands you the screen. You do that one step and press I'm done.

How much of my Claude plan does it use?

Bots work in rotating shifts sized to what's left of your plan: up to three at once when you have room, one when you're running low, and they pause before you hit the limit. Bosses with questions waiting go first, so nobody sits blocked.

Can my cofounder use it too?

Yes. Under Settings, Team, add them to get a private sign-in link (it works over Tailscale or the same Wi-Fi). Their requests carry their name and the Bots' questions go to them; only you can send from your accounts or change settings. Since a Claude plan is for one person, switch to a company Anthropic API key when you share.

Does it work when my laptop is closed?

Bots run on your Mac, so they work whenever it's awake. Tandem starts at login and keeps going with the window closed. For round-the-clock routines, run it on a Mac mini that stays on.

Can I use it from my phone?

Yes. Run tandem install --lan, open the link under Settings, Devices on your phone, and add it to your home screen.

Who makes this?

It's an independent open-source project, not affiliated with Anthropic, xAI or SpaceX. Contributions are welcome.
